## Topic

How can an AI with local knowledge answer questions about reusing spent coffee grounds from Bangkok cafés?

Bangkok has thousands of cafés, and most of their used coffee grounds go to waste. General AI chatbots know a lot about coffee in general, but very little about local cafés, Thai research and what is actually possible in Bangkok. We want to see if giving the AI our own local documents makes its answers more accurate and more useful.

## What "AI with local knowledge" means

Instead of only using what the AI learned during training, we give it a set of our own documents. When we ask a question, the AI looks through these documents first and uses them in its answer. This should help it give answers that fit the local context.

## Plan

| Step | What we will do |
|---|---|
| 1. Collect local documents | Café information, Thai and international research on spent coffee grounds, and our own project notes |
| 2. Set up the AI | Load our documents into the AI setup from class |
| 3. Write test questions | For example: Which uses of spent coffee grounds work best for small Bangkok cafés? |
| 4. Compare answers | Ask the same questions to the AI with our documents and a normal AI without them |
| 5. Report findings | What improved, what did not, and what we learned |
